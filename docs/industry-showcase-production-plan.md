# Velari Industry Showcase — Production Plan

## Goal

Turn the second section from a stylish animation containing generalized panels into a credible proof wall: nine responsive industry concepts that show both the public-facing website and the operating system behind it.

The Animos sequence remains the cinematic entrance. It is not expected to carry every detail by itself. After the animation, visitors should be able to pause on each industry, inspect the desktop composition, and switch to a real phone view.

## Final industry set

1. Property
2. Dental
3. Restaurant
4. Trades
5. Industrial
6. Logistics
7. Retail
8. Research Labs
9. Construction

Restaurant replaces the broad “Hospitality” label. Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC are represented within Trades. Retail and Research Labs receive separate concepts so neither fashion commerce nor the existing peptide/lab work is buried. Construction adds a dedicated project-control direction for general contractors and commercial builders.

## Non-negotiable quality gates

Every concept must:

- Be built as responsive HTML/CSS, not generated as a complete raster UI.
- Have a distinct layout, typography system, component geometry, and color language.
- Pair a convincing public landing with three or four operational proofs.
- Use concrete, industry-specific copy and realistic sample data.
- Work at a 1920×1080 Animos capture without important content entering the outer crop zone.
- Work at a 390×844 phone viewport with no horizontal page overflow.
- Recompose for mobile; never merely shrink or center-crop the desktop scene.
- Keep primary claims, headings, and module labels readable while the animation is moving.
- Pass a desktop capture, phone capture, console check, and crop review before entering Animos.

Generated imagery is limited to photography, product imagery, textures, or other art assets. Interface text, forms, maps, controls, and operational modules remain code-native.

## Concept briefs

### 01 — Property

Public face:

- Quiet-luxury residence landing
- Availability and private-tour CTA
- Neighborhood and resident-service positioning

System proofs:

- Applicant verification and move-in workflow
- Rent collection and payment health
- Maintenance ticket assignment
- Occupancy and renewal pulse

Visual language:

- Midnight navy, bronze, warm ivory
- Editorial serif typography
- Translucent resident-operation modules
- Mobile bottom dock and horizontally scrollable service cards

### 02 — Dental

Public face:

- Luminous, calming practice landing
- Provider credibility and treatment philosophy
- Prominent new-patient and appointment actions

System proofs:

- AI-assisted new-patient intake
- Insurance and eligibility verification
- Appointment calendar and reminders
- Treatment-plan approval and payment options

Visual language:

- Bright mineral white, soft cobalt, pale mint
- Clinical grid with generous whitespace
- iOS-style intake sheets and progress states
- No dark-glass reuse from Property

### 03 — Restaurant

Public face:

- Editorial food and dining landing
- Menu storytelling, hours, location, and reservation CTA

System proofs:

- Reservation pacing and table map
- Online ordering and kitchen handoff
- Guest preferences and return visits
- Shift or service-volume snapshot

Visual language:

- Bone, oxblood, smoked espresso, copper
- Magazine-like crop and expressive typography
- Tactile ticket and table components
- Mobile-first reservation flow

### 04 — Trades

Public face:

- High-conversion local service landing
- Emergency availability, reviews, service area, and clear quote CTA

System proofs:

- AI lead intake and job qualification
- Dispatch board and technician assignment
- Route and arrival updates
- Estimate, invoice, and payment status

Visual language:

- Utility blue, safety orange, warm gray
- Bold sans typography and unmistakable actions
- Field-ready cards rather than luxury glass
- Phone layout optimized for tap-to-call and quote completion

### 05 — Industrial

Public face:

- Capabilities and facility landing
- RFQ path, certifications, and production confidence

System proofs:

- RFQ intake and quoting
- Machine or line status
- Preventive-maintenance timeline
- Inventory, quality, and safety signals

Visual language:

- Graphite, concrete, signal yellow
- Industrial grotesk and technical annotations
- Strong rules, grids, and measured density
- No consumer-app softness

### 06 — Logistics

Public face:

- Network, service-region, and shipment-confidence landing
- Quote and shipment-tracking actions

System proofs:

- Live load map and route progress
- Dispatch and driver assignment
- Exception alerts
- Proof of delivery and customer updates

Visual language:

- Deep navy, cloud white, route cyan
- Map-led composition with restrained SaaS clarity
- Compact live-status elements
- Phone view prioritizes tracking and exception resolution

### 07 — Retail

Public face:

- Fashion or design-led commerce landing
- Campaign storytelling, collection navigation, and product discovery

System proofs:

- Cart and accelerated checkout
- Inventory and low-stock visibility
- Orders, returns, and fulfillment
- Customer loyalty and back-in-stock automation

Visual language:

- Editorial cream, ink black, one seasonal accent
- Product-first layout with large typographic rhythm
- Polished commerce sheets instead of dashboard panels
- True thumb-friendly product and checkout views

### 08 — Research Labs

Public face:

- High-trust research-commerce landing
- Product discovery, open research dossiers, and member access

System proofs:

- Account terms and compliant checkout
- Inventory and fulfillment status
- Order records, invoices, and shipping
- Product dossier and research-document access

Visual language:

- Near-black, laboratory gray, champagne gold
- Scientific editorial system and disciplined glass
- Molecular or dossier detail used with restraint
- No unsupported medical claims or consumer-health positioning

### 09 — Construction

Public face:

- Commercial-builder landing with a clear preconstruction and project-start action
- Credibility through jobsite photography, schedule status, and accountable delivery

System proofs:

- Master schedule and phase completion
- RFI response and field notification
- Change-order review and allowance tracking
- Pay applications, lien waivers, and release status

Visual language:

- Mineral gray, warm timber, off-white, and restrained rust red
- Architectural grid with assertive uppercase typography
- Squared project-control cards instead of consumer-app glass
- Mobile view preserves the jobsite story and exposes swipeable field records

## Capture and integration pipeline

For each industry:

1. Produce or select the hero/product imagery.
2. Build the responsive concept page.
3. Capture the 1920×1080 Animos-safe frame.
4. Capture the 390×844 phone viewport and a full mobile page.
5. Fix crop, type size, and density problems.
6. Export a clean PNG for the appropriate Animos slot.
7. Add the concept to the post-animation gallery with desktop/phone viewing controls.

Only after all nine pass their individual quality gates should the final Animos MP4 be regenerated and the old generalized panels retired.
